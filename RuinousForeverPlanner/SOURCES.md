## Ten-level efficiency / five-player data (reviewed October 20, 2026)
https://foreverchanges.pro/dungeons and its 35 linked detail pages: factual level bands, quest IDs, faction, beta reference XP and reward/drop item IDs only. The offline compiler excludes Upper Blackrock Spire; 34 dungeon/wing rows, 331 quest rows and 2,058 reward/drop rows remain. Quest XP records without beta evidence remain unknown. Classic dungeon bands are expressly unconfirmed in Forever. No source prose, upstream addon code, assumed drop odds or clear times are redistributed. Cached inputs and compiler are in the source archive.
https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-classic/leveling-guide reviewed for the general policy of batching objectives, accounting for travel/group quality, and distinguishing prepared quest runs from prolonged farming; exact policy thresholds are RFP heuristics.
C_Map.GetAreaInfo, GetRealZoneText, UnitXP, UnitXPMax, GetTime, C_TooltipInfo.GetHyperlink and PLAYER_XP_UPDATE were verified against the supplied Forever API export. English base-DPS text parsing is guarded and not guaranteed in a live client. No character-DPS simulation or ten-level time-to-level forecast is claimed.

Enchant and off-hand correction (v0.18): https://foreverchanges.pro/professions/enchanting supplies 165 factual enchant records and published-profile associations. 103 effects are recognized static numeric stats; others remain explicitly unscored. Candidate enchant benchmarks use only matching published level-30 profile references, not arbitrary level eligibility inferred from maker skill. Base-versus-exact item-link stat reads prevent double counting; a guarded English permanent-enchant tooltip parser is the fallback. ItemEnchantmentPermanent is documented in the Forever UI source: https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/forever/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/TooltipInfoSharedDocumentation.lua . The import correction recognizes slot-held, slot-shield, slot-two-hand and slot-relic; unknown future slot-* sections now stop the crawl rather than silently disappearing. Current crawl: 38 lists, 634 item records (621 source-confirmed), 111 linked quest URLs, 110 accessible pages; quest 79242 returns 404. This is source verification, not live client testing.
